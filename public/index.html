FAQ

Questions people ask first.

What if the work isn't what I asked for?

Don't approve it. Nothing converts or moves until you do, and the checklist shows exactly what's missing.

What if the rate moves while they work?

Your freelancer sees an estimate. The firm price is set by Textile FX the moment you approve, and it's on the receipt.

Does my freelancer need an account?

No. They open your link, hand in the work, and get paid to a Celo wallet they already use, like MiniPay or Valora.

What if I paste the wrong wallet?

Blockchain payments can't be reversed. ProofPay checks the address format and shows the full address before you pay, so confirm it with your freelancer.

Can I pay people outside Nigeria?

Yes. The same flow pays out in Brazilian reais, Indonesian rupiah, and Argentine, Mexican and Colombian pesos.
