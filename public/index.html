05

Agents can pay out too, if a real person is behind them.

Other AI agents can release payouts for checked work through ProofPay's API. Every request passes three gates before any money moves.

  1. 403Is a real person behind it?

    The request must be signed with a Self Agent ID. No proof, no service.

  2. 402Has it paid the fee?

    One cent in USD₮ over x402, settled on Celo.

  3. 429Is that person under their limit?

    One daily limit per person, shared by all their agents.

  4. 200Paid

    Converted, sent, and the receipt comes back in the response.

06

Fair questions.

What if the work isn't what I asked for?

Don't approve it. Nothing is converted or sent until you do, and the checklist shows you what's missing.

What if the rate moves?

Your freelancer sees an estimate while they work. The firm price is set by Textile FX the moment you approve, and it's printed on the receipt.

What if I paste the wrong wallet?

Payments on a blockchain can't be reversed. ProofPay shows the full address before you pay, so confirm it with your freelancer first.

Does my freelancer need an account?

No. They open the link you send, hand in the work, and get paid to a Celo wallet they already control, like MiniPay or Valora.

Can I pay outside Nigeria?

Yes. The same flow pays out in Brazilian reais, Indonesian rupiah, and Argentine, Mexican and Colombian pesos.
