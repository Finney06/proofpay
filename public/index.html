FAQ

Questions people ask first.

What if the work isn't what I asked for?

Don't approve it. Nothing is converted or sent until you do, and the checklist shows you what's missing.

What if the rate moves while they work?

Your freelancer sees an estimate. The firm price is set by Textile FX when you approve, and it's on the receipt.

Does my freelancer need an account?

No. They open your link, hand in the work, and get paid to a Celo wallet they already control, such as MiniPay or Valora.

What if I paste the wrong wallet address?

Payments on a blockchain can't be reversed. ProofPay shows the full address before you pay, so confirm it with your freelancer first.

Can I pay people outside Nigeria?

Yes. The same flow pays out in Brazilian reais, Indonesian rupiah, and Argentine, Mexican and Colombian pesos.
